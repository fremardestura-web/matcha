03 / THE FORMULA

Build a better
matcha latte.

  1. Whisk. Sift 1–2 tsp matcha. Add 50–70 ml warm water, around 70–80°C, then whisk until smooth and lightly foamy.
  2. Balance. Add 10–20 ml syrup or sweetener if your milk and toppings are unsweetened.
  3. Layer. Add ice, milk and fruit or foam slowly for clean café-style layers.
  4. Finish. Dust with matcha, add fruit, a cloud of cold foam, or your favorite crunchy topping.
✿

KAIEN tip: Avoid boiling water. Very hot water can make matcha taste harsher and more bitter.
